# Prepclose — Business Summary & Sales Pitch

## What Prepclose does

Your reps are losing deals they should be winning — not because they can't sell, but because they're showing up to calls cold. They don't know the company's funding situation, they missed the news about the CEO change last week, they have no idea the prospect just posted 4 AE roles. So the call starts generic, the prospect feels like a number, and the deal dies before it starts.

The problem isn't effort. Reps want to prep. They just don't have time — 30 minutes of research per call adds up fast, and when the calendar's packed, research is the first thing that gets skipped.

Prepclose fixes that. The moment a lead enters your pipeline, we automatically pull everything — company background, funding stage, tech stack, hiring signals, recent news, who you're talking to and what they care about — and deliver a full brief to Slack or email before the call. Your reps walk in knowing more than the prospect expects. No manual work. No skipped prep. Every call, every rep.
